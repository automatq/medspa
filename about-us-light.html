Advanced aesthetics in Etobicoke

A clinic in Lakeshore Village.

Anima Med Spa is a medical aesthetics clinic in Etobicoke’s Lakeshore Village, focused on advanced non-surgical cosmetic and wellness treatments.

The menu spans skin, laser, injectable, wellness, and permanent makeup services, delivered by trained professionals with medical-grade products and devices.

  • Skin rejuvenation
  • Cosmetic injectables
  • Weight management
  • Chemical peels
  • Microblading
