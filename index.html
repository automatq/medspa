Your questions, answered

Before you book anything.

01What types of treatments do you offer?

Medical-grade facials, chemical peels, neuromodulators and dermal fillers, laser and light-based treatments, body and wellness programs, and brow, lash and permanent makeup services. The full list is on the treatments page.

02Who performs the treatments, and do I need a consultation?

Advanced treatments are performed or overseen by trained professionals, and a consultation comes first. It reviews your goals, health history, and skin so the plan suits you specifically. You can meet the team on the about page.

03How should I prepare for a first visit?

Arrive with clean skin and a list of any medications and skincare products you use. Your provider will review your history, look at your skin, talk through the options, and treat the same day only if that is appropriate.

04Will there be downtime or side effects?

It depends on the treatment. Many have little to no downtime, but temporary redness, swelling, or sensitivity is common and expected. Your provider explains the likely response and gives written aftercare before you go ahead.

05How do I book, reschedule, or cancel?

Book through the live schedule, by phone, or through social channels. To change or cancel an appointment, contact the clinic within its stated notice period to avoid a fee.
