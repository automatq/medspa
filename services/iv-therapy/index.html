IV Therapy from the inside out.

A medically supervised intravenous infusion selected after health screening to provide hydration and a defined blend of nutrients.

What it supports

Goals worth discussing clearly.

  • Hydration support
  • Selected nutrient support
  • Travel or recovery routines
  • General wellness goals

IV therapy introduces fluids and selected nutrients directly through an intravenous line. The formulation must be matched to the client after medical screening rather than chosen only by a marketing name.

It is not a substitute for diagnosis, emergency care, a balanced diet, or treatment of a medical condition. Potential benefits and risks depend on the ingredients, dose, and individual health profile.

Your appointment

A considered process, step by step.

  1. 01

    Screen

    A medical history and suitability review determines whether an infusion is appropriate.

  2. 02

    Select

    The provider confirms the formula, ingredients, and purpose of the planned infusion.

  3. 03

    Infuse

    A small IV line is placed and the solution is administered while you are monitored.

  4. 04

    Review

    The site and your response are checked before discharge and any future plan.

Potential benefits

What this treatment may support.

Benefits describe treatment goals, not guaranteed outcomes. Your provider will explain what is realistic for you.

  • Direct route for hydration and selected nutrients
  • Formula is selected after screening
  • Monitored appointment
  • Can fit into a broader clinician-guided wellness plan

Before you book

Candidacy, preparation, and care after.

01

Who it may suit

A medical screening is essential. Kidney, heart, blood-pressure, electrolyte, pregnancy, medication, allergy, and other health considerations may affect eligibility.

02

How to prepare

Provide a complete health and medication history, eat and hydrate as directed, and ask for the exact ingredients and doses before treatment.

03

Downtime & response

Temporary bruising, tenderness, bleeding, or irritation at the IV site can occur. Systemic risks vary with the formula and medical history.

04

Aftercare

Keep the IV site clean and monitor for persistent pain, swelling, redness, drainage, dizziness, breathing changes, or other unexpected symptoms.

Consultation matters: A consultation is required to confirm suitability, treatment scope, expected downtime, and current pricing.

Your questions, answered

IV Therapy FAQ.

01What is IV therapy?

It is the delivery of fluids and selected nutrients through an intravenous line after medical screening.

02How long does an appointment take?

Anima’s current page lists a typical infusion time of 30 to 60 minutes.

03Is it medically supervised?

Anima states that its IV treatments are administered under medical supervision.

04Can IV therapy treat an illness?

This page is not medical diagnosis or emergency guidance. Discuss symptoms and health conditions with an appropriate healthcare professional.

05How do I know what is in the infusion?

Ask the provider to review every ingredient, dose, intended purpose, alternative, and relevant risk before you consent.
