Injectable treatments · Targeted micro-infusion

Mesotherapy precisely delivered.

A series of targeted micro-injections selected to deliver a customized formulation for skin hydration, texture, pigmentation, or scalp support.

What it supports

Goals worth discussing clearly.

  • Skin dehydration
  • Dullness
  • Uneven texture
  • Selected scalp or body goals

Mesotherapy uses multiple small injections to place a selected formulation into the treatment area. Anima’s current page describes blends that may include vitamins, minerals, antioxidants, or hyaluronic acid.

There is no universal mesotherapy formula. Ingredients, depth, area, and session plan must be confirmed for the specific goal and client.

Your appointment

A considered process, step by step.

  1. 01

    Assess

    The provider reviews skin or scalp goals, health history, sensitivities, and treatment area.

  2. 02

    Select

    A formulation and injection plan are chosen for the intended use.

  3. 03

    Infuse

    Small micro-injections are placed across the selected area.

  4. 04

    Support

    Skin settles and a treatment series may be recommended based on response.

Potential benefits

What this treatment may support.

Benefits describe treatment goals, not guaranteed outcomes. Your provider will explain what is realistic for you.

  • Highly targeted treatment approach
  • Formulation can be selected for the concern
  • May support hydration and texture
  • Minimal interruption for many clients

Before you book

Candidacy, preparation, and care after.

01

Who it may suit

Suitability depends on the treatment formulation, medical history, allergies, pregnancy status, medicines, active skin conditions, and treatment goal.

02

How to prepare

Ask exactly which ingredients are planned and disclose allergies, medications, supplements, pregnancy or breastfeeding, prior reactions, and active skin or scalp issues.

03

Downtime & response

Temporary redness, swelling, tenderness, pinpoint marks, or bruising can occur. The recovery window varies by area and formulation.

04

Aftercare

Use only products approved for the treated area, avoid friction and heat as directed, and protect exposed skin from sun.

Consultation matters: A consultation is required to confirm suitability, treatment scope, expected downtime, and current pricing.

Your questions, answered

Mesotherapy FAQ.

01What is mesotherapy?

It is a micro-injection technique used to place selected ingredients into a targeted skin, scalp, or body area.

02Which areas can be treated?

Anima lists the face, scalp, and body, depending on the goal and formulation.

03What is in the treatment?

There is no single formula. Ask the provider to confirm every ingredient and why it is appropriate for your plan.

04How many sessions will I need?

Anima advises that a series is often recommended. The exact number and spacing vary.

05Is numbing available?

The current page notes that numbing can be used when appropriate. Comfort options are confirmed during consultation.
