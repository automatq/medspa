Intimate Brightening handled with discretion.

A professional Pink Intimate® topical protocol for selected external areas, offered with privacy, clear suitability screening, and careful aftercare.

What it supports

Goals worth discussing clearly.

  • Uneven tone in selected external areas
  • Friction-related darkening
  • Tone changes after hair removal
  • Discreet professional care

Anima’s current service uses the Pink Intimate® professional system, a topical protocol intended for selected external intimate or high-friction areas.

Not every colour change is cosmetic. New, changing, symptomatic, or unexplained pigmentation should be assessed by an appropriate healthcare professional before aesthetic treatment.

A considered process, step by step.

  1. 01

    Private consultation

  2. 02

  4. 04

Potential benefits

What this treatment may support.

Benefits describe treatment goals, not guaranteed outcomes. Your provider will explain what is realistic for you.

  • Professional protocol for selected external areas
  • Discreet consultation and treatment
  • No invasive device or injection
  • Aftercare tailored to a friction-prone area

Before you book

Candidacy, preparation, and care after.

01

Who it may suit

Only selected external skin areas are considered. Active irritation, infection, lesions, pregnancy, recent hair removal, unexplained pigment change, or ingredient sensitivity may require deferral or medical review.

02

How to prepare

Follow the clinic’s directions on shaving, waxing, deodorants, fragrance, exfoliation, and topical products. Report any itch, pain, discharge, lesion, or recent change.

03

Downtime & response

Anima describes no downtime, but temporary sensitivity or irritation is possible. Friction and heat may need to be limited.

04

Aftercare

Use only approved external products, keep the area cool and dry, reduce friction as directed, and stop use if irritation develops.

Consultation matters: A consultation is required to confirm suitability, treatment scope, expected downtime, and current pricing.

Your questions, answered

Intimate Brightening FAQ.

01Which areas can be treated?

Only selected external areas approved by the provider and product protocol. Internal tissue is not treated.

02Is the service permanent?

No cosmetic brightening result should be considered permanent. Friction, hormones, hair removal, and skin biology continue to affect tone.

03Is there downtime?

Anima describes no planned downtime, although sensitivity can occur and aftercare restrictions still matter.

04When should I seek medical assessment first?

New, changing, painful, itchy, raised, bleeding, or otherwise unexplained colour changes should be medically assessed.

05Can I wax or shave before treatment?

Timing must follow the clinic’s instructions because recent hair removal can increase irritation risk.
