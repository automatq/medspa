Dermal Fillers with restraint.

A personalized injectable treatment for restoring selected volume, refining contours, and supporting facial balance without losing what looks like you.

What it supports

Goals worth discussing clearly.

  • Volume changes
  • Lip definition
  • Cheek or chin contour
  • Facial balance

Dermal fillers are injectable products used to add or restore volume in selected areas. Anima’s current service page identifies the lips, cheeks, chin, jawline, and under-eye area as possible treatment zones.

Product, placement, and amount are determined only after facial assessment. A conservative plan can be staged over time rather than trying to create every change in one appointment.

Your appointment

A considered process, step by step.

  1. 01

    Assess

    Facial structure, movement, medical history, and your priorities are reviewed.

  2. 02

    Plan

    The provider recommends areas, product, amount, and an appropriate treatment sequence.

  3. 03

    Treat

    Filler is placed using a technique selected for the area and intended effect.

  4. 04

    Settle

    Swelling and bruising are monitored while the product integrates over the following days.

Potential benefits

What this treatment may support.

Benefits describe treatment goals, not guaranteed outcomes. Your provider will explain what is realistic for you.

  • May restore selected facial volume
  • Can refine lips or facial contours
  • Treatment is tailored to proportion and anatomy
  • Changes are visible without surgery

Before you book

Candidacy, preparation, and care after.

01

Who it may suit

Suitability depends on your medical history, skin condition, anatomy, treatment area, and expectations. Injectable filler is not appropriate for everyone and requires an in-person assessment.

02

How to prepare

Share all medications, supplements, allergies, dental work, illness, previous fillers, and upcoming events. Your provider will give specific pre-treatment directions.

03

Downtime & response

Swelling, redness, tenderness, and bruising can occur and may last several days. Timing varies by area, product, amount, and individual response.

04

Aftercare

Follow the provider’s guidance on pressure, exercise, heat, alcohol, skincare, and makeup. Seek urgent medical advice for severe pain, colour change, vision symptoms, or other unexpected reactions.

Consultation matters: A consultation is required to confirm suitability, treatment scope, expected downtime, and current pricing.

Your questions, answered

Dermal Fillers FAQ.

01Which areas can be treated?

Anima’s current page lists lips, cheeks, chin, jawline, and under-eyes. Your provider will confirm which areas are appropriate for you.

02Will the result look overfilled?

The intended approach is subtle facial balance. Product choice and amount are discussed before treatment, and a staged plan may be recommended.

03How long can filler last?

Anima lists a broad range of roughly six to eighteen months. Longevity depends on product, area, metabolism, movement, and treatment amount.

04What downtime should I plan for?

Temporary swelling, tenderness, redness, and bruising are possible. Allow flexibility before important events.

05How much filler will I need?

There is no reliable one-size answer. Amount is confirmed after assessing anatomy, volume change, and the result you want.
