Injectable treatments · Collagen-stimulating injectable

Sculptra® gradually restored.

A collagen-stimulating injectable designed for progressive, subtle change rather than instant volume—planned around facial structure and long-term goals.

What it supports

Goals worth discussing clearly.

  • Gradual volume change
  • Skin firmness
  • Facial contour
  • Overall skin quality

Sculptra® contains poly-L-lactic acid (PLLA) and is used to stimulate collagen production beneath the skin. Unlike traditional filler, the intended change develops progressively as collagen support rebuilds.

Treatment areas, number of sessions, and maintenance depend on anatomy and goals. Immediate fullness after treatment can reflect fluid and swelling rather than the final effect.

A considered process, step by step.

Potential benefits

What this treatment may support.

Benefits describe treatment goals, not guaranteed outcomes. Your provider will explain what is realistic for you.

  • Supports natural collagen production
  • Gradual rather than abrupt change
  • May improve firmness and facial support
  • Can be planned as a staged treatment

Before you book

Candidacy, preparation, and care after.

01

Who it may suit

May suit adults seeking progressive collagen support and facial volume restoration. Medical history, autoimmune conditions, active skin issues, and expectations must be reviewed.

02

How to prepare

Share all health conditions, medicines, supplements, allergies, previous injectables, and event timing. Follow the injector’s treatment-specific preparation.

03

Downtime & response

Temporary swelling, tenderness, redness, or bruising may occur. The provider will explain how the immediate post-treatment appearance differs from the developing result.

04

Aftercare

Follow the exact massage and care instructions provided for your treatment; do not rely on generic online protocols.

Consultation matters: A consultation is required to confirm suitability, treatment scope, expected downtime, and current pricing.

Your questions, answered

Sculptra FAQ.

01How is Sculptra different from filler?

Sculptra is intended to stimulate collagen gradually, while traditional fillers add volume more directly. The right option depends on the goal and anatomy.

02When do changes appear?

The current Anima page says results develop gradually over several weeks to months.

03How many sessions will I need?

A series spaced several weeks apart is commonly recommended, but your provider confirms the number after assessment.

04Why do I look fuller right after treatment?

Early fullness can come from fluid and temporary swelling. It is not the final collagen-stimulating result.

05How long can results last?

Anima states that results may last up to two years for some clients. Individual response and maintenance vary.
