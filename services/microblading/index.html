Permanent makeup · Semi-permanent brow artistry

Microblading mapped to your features.

A semi-permanent cosmetic tattoo technique using fine, hair-like strokes to add the appearance of brow density and definition.

What it supports

Goals worth discussing clearly.

  • Sparse-looking brows
  • Uneven brow shape
  • Daily brow-pencil routine
  • Hair-stroke definition

Microblading deposits cosmetic pigment into superficial skin using a manual blade formed from very fine needles. The goal is to create hair-like strokes that complement natural growth.

It is a form of cosmetic tattooing. Pigment fades and can shift over time, and healed results differ from the darker, sharper appearance immediately after treatment.

Your appointment

A considered process, step by step.

  1. 01

    Consult

    Skin type, medical history, prior tattooing, goals, and maintenance expectations are reviewed.

  2. 02

    Map

    Brow shape and symmetry are measured and approved before pigment placement.

  3. 03

    Blade

    Fine strokes are created using the selected pigment and technique.

  4. 04

    Heal

    The brows soften through a staged healing process before a touch-up is considered.

Potential benefits

What this treatment may support.

Benefits describe treatment goals, not guaranteed outcomes. Your provider will explain what is realistic for you.

  • Hair-stroke appearance
  • Shape is mapped before treatment
  • Can reduce daily brow filling
  • Semi-permanent rather than daily makeup

Before you book

Candidacy, preparation, and care after.

01

Who it may suit

Suitability depends on skin type, healing history, medicines, pregnancy, active skin conditions, prior pigment, scar tendency, and expectations. Oily skin may retain strokes differently.

02

How to prepare

Follow directions for brow hair removal, retinoids, exfoliation, sun, alcohol, caffeine, medicines, and supplements. Do not stop prescriptions without medical advice.

03

Downtime & response

Brows can look dark or sharply defined at first, followed by dryness, flaking, and uneven-looking fading during healing.

04

Aftercare

Follow the artist’s exact wet-or-dry healing protocol, avoid picking, sweating, swimming, sun, and unapproved products, and schedule touch-up only after full healing.

Consultation matters: A consultation is required to confirm suitability, treatment scope, expected downtime, and current pricing.

Your questions, answered

Microblading FAQ.

01Is microblading a tattoo?

Yes. It is a semi-permanent cosmetic tattoo technique that places pigment into superficial skin.

02How will the brows look right away?

They usually look darker and more defined initially, then soften and may appear patchy during healing.

03Will I need a touch-up?

An initial touch-up is commonly part of the process after the skin has fully healed.

04How long does it last?

Longevity varies with skin type, pigment, sun exposure, skincare, technique, and maintenance.

05Can old brow tattooing be covered?

Sometimes, but colour, depth, shape, and scar tissue must be assessed before accepting the service.
