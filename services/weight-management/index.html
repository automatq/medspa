Weight Management Program supported, not rushed.

A clinician-guided weight-management program built around medical history, daily habits, barriers, sustainable goals, and ongoing follow-up.

What it supports

Goals worth discussing clearly.

  • Structured weight support
  • Lifestyle and habit barriers
  • Plateaus and accountability
  • Medication eligibility assessment

Anima describes a medically supervised program focused on health history, habits, goals, barriers, and appropriate follow-up. Medication eligibility may be evaluated, but medication is not assumed or guaranteed.

Weight is influenced by medical, psychological, social, sleep, nutrition, activity, and medication factors. The program should support informed, sustainable care rather than extreme short-term change.

Your appointment

A considered process, step by step.

  1. 01

    Medical intake

    Health history, medicines, prior attempts, symptoms, habits, and goals are reviewed.

  2. 02

    Assessment

    The clinician identifies barriers, relevant measurements, and whether further testing or referral is needed.

  3. 03

    Plan

    A personalized plan may include nutrition, activity, behaviour support, monitoring, and medication assessment.

  4. 04

    Follow up

    Progress, tolerability, challenges, and next steps are reviewed over time.

Potential benefits

What this treatment may support.

Benefits describe treatment goals, not guaranteed outcomes. Your provider will explain what is realistic for you.

  • Medical oversight
  • Plan accounts for health history and lifestyle
  • Ongoing monitoring and accountability
  • Medication eligibility can be assessed when appropriate

Before you book

Candidacy, preparation, and care after.

01

Who it may suit

A consultation is required to determine whether the program is appropriate. Health history, pregnancy, medications, eating-disorder history, mental health, and individual risk factors must be considered.

02

How to prepare

Bring an accurate medication and supplement list, relevant medical history, prior weight-management approaches, allergies, recent laboratory results if available, and your questions.

03

Downtime & response

There is no procedure downtime for consultation. Any treatment-specific effects, risks, and monitoring requirements are discussed before starting a plan.

04

Aftercare

Attend follow-ups, report side effects or major health changes, and seek urgent care for emergency symptoms. Do not change prescribed medication without your clinician.

Consultation matters: A consultation is required to confirm suitability, treatment scope, expected downtime, and current pricing.

Your questions, answered

Weight Management Program FAQ.

01What makes the program medically supervised?

Anima states that a healthcare professional designs and monitors the plan, tracks progress, and adjusts care based on response.

02Does everyone receive weight-loss medication?

No. Medication eligibility is assessed individually and is only one possible part of a broader plan.

03How quickly will I see results?

Anima emphasizes that results vary. The goal is steady, sustainable progress rather than an extreme fixed timeline.

04Will there be ongoing support?

Yes. The current page describes monitoring and follow-up as part of the program.

05Am I a candidate?

A medical consultation is required to review health history, goals, risks, contraindications, and appropriate alternatives.
