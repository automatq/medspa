Lip Shading softly tinted.

A semi-permanent cosmetic tattoo that layers custom-blended pigment into the lips to even out tone, redefine faded borders, and reduce reliance on daily lip colour.

What it supports

Goals worth discussing clearly.

  • Pale or washed-out lip colour
  • Uneven lip pigmentation
  • Blurred or faded lip borders
  • Daily lipstick or liner routine

Lip shading is a cosmetic tattooing technique that deposits medical-grade pigment into the lips using gentle shading passes. Anima’s current page describes it as a custom-blended semi-permanent tint used to correct pale tones, even out uneven pigmentation, and sharpen blurred borders.

Intensity is chosen with you, from a subtle lip blush to a more defined finish. The effect comes from colour and border definition rather than added volume, and the healed tone is softer than the vivid colour visible immediately after the appointment.

Your appointment

A considered process, step by step.

  1. 01

    Consult

    Lip shape, natural tone, medical history, and the colour and intensity you want are reviewed and mapped.

  2. 02

    Numb

    A topical numbing product is applied for comfort before pigment is placed.

  3. 03

    Shade

    Pigment is layered into the lips in gentle passes to build the agreed tone.

  4. 04

    Heal

    Lips look bright and slightly swollen at first, then flake and soften before the touch-up is planned.

Potential benefits

What this treatment may support.

Benefits describe treatment goals, not guaranteed outcomes. Your provider will explain what is realistic for you.

  • May even out faded or uneven lip tone
  • Can restore definition to blurred borders
  • Colour is custom blended to your preference
  • Less reliance on daily lipstick or liner

Before you book

Candidacy, preparation, and care after.

01

Who it may suit

May suit adults who want more colour or clearer lip definition without added volume. Cold-sore history, pregnancy, breastfeeding, medicines, allergies, active lip conditions, previous lip pigment, and scar tendency all affect suitability and must be reviewed in person.

02

How to prepare

Share your medical history, medicines, supplements, allergies, cold-sore history, and any previous lip pigment at consultation. If you have had lip filler, Anima advises leaving at least four weeks before a lip shading session.

03

Downtime & response

Anima notes that lips look vibrant, bright, and slightly swollen immediately afterward, and that the initial bold colour flakes away within roughly three to five days to reveal a softer tint. Allow flexibility before important events.

04

Aftercare

Follow the artist’s exact lip-healing instructions, keep the lips clean and protected, and do not pick flaking pigment. Report unexpected swelling, blistering, or possible signs of infection to the clinic promptly.

Consultation matters: A consultation is required to confirm suitability, treatment scope, expected downtime, and current pricing.

Your questions, answered

Lip Shading FAQ.

01Will the colour smudge or transfer?

No. The pigment sits within the skin rather than on the surface, so it does not rub off on cups or clothing the way lipstick does.

02How long does healing take?

Anima says the initial bold colour flakes off within about three to five days, leaving the softer tint underneath.

03How long do results last?

Anima lists roughly two to three years depending on lifestyle, metabolism, and sun exposure, with a touch-up 6 to 8 weeks after the first visit and maintenance suggested every 18 to 24 months.

04Can I have lip filler as well?

Anima says the two can be combined, but advises leaving at least four weeks between a filler appointment and a lip shading session.

05Will my lips look fuller?

Not physically. Any change in apparent fullness comes from colour correction and border definition, not added volume.
