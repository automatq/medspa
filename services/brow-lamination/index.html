Beauty enhancements · Soft-set brow styling

Brow Lamination fuller in form.

A non-permanent brow styling service that relaxes and resets natural hairs into a lifted, more uniform shape.

Typical session
Typically 45–60 minutes
Downtime
None expected
Treatment plan
Repeat around 6–8 weeks
Consultation
Brief intake

What it supports

Goals worth discussing clearly.

  • Downward-growing brow hairs
  • Uneven direction
  • Sparse-looking areas
  • A brushed-up brow style

Brow lamination uses a controlled setting process to soften and redirect natural brow hairs. Shaping and conditioning complete the service.

It changes hair direction temporarily; it does not add hair or create permanent pigment. The final look can range from softly groomed to more feathered.

Your appointment

A considered process, step by step.

  1. 01

    Design

    Natural growth, skin condition, desired shape, and product history are reviewed.

  2. 02

    Lift

    A softening solution allows hairs to be repositioned.

  3. 03

    Set

    Brows are brushed into the approved direction and fixed in place.

  4. 04

    Condition

    Shaping and a nourishing finish complete the service.

Potential benefits

What this treatment may support.

Benefits describe treatment goals, not guaranteed outcomes. Your provider will explain what is realistic for you.

  • Fuller-looking natural brows
  • Improves uniformity and direction
  • No cosmetic tattooing
  • Low daily styling effort

Before you book

Candidacy, preparation, and care after.

01

Who it may suit

May suit healthy brow hair and intact surrounding skin. Active dermatitis, broken skin, severe sensitivity, overprocessed hairs, or prior product reaction can make treatment unsuitable.

02

How to prepare

Avoid unapproved exfoliation, retinoids, waxing, or tinting near the appointment and disclose allergies, irritation, and prior reactions.

03

Downtime & response

No downtime is expected. Temporary redness or sensitivity can occur.

04

Aftercare

Keep brows dry and avoid steam, heavy skincare, oils, and rubbing for the technician’s specified period, commonly 24 hours.

Consultation matters: A consultation is required to confirm suitability, treatment scope, expected downtime, and current pricing.

Your questions, answered

Brow Lamination FAQ.

01Does brow lamination add hair?

No. It repositions existing brow hairs so they can look fuller and more uniform.

02How long does it last?

Anima lists approximately six to eight weeks, depending on hair cycle and aftercare.

03Can it be paired with tint?

Yes, tint can add depth when suitable. Product tolerance and timing are confirmed during the service.

04Will it damage my brows?

Correct product timing and conditioning reduce risk, but overprocessing is possible. Hair and skin condition must be checked.

05What should I avoid afterward?

Avoid water, steam, rubbing, and heavy products around the brows during the initial aftercare window.
