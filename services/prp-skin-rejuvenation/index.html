PRP Skin Rejuvenation from within.

A treatment using platelet-rich plasma prepared from your own blood to support collagen activity, skin texture, and gradual rejuvenation.

Typical session
Typically under 60 minutes
Downtime
Redness or swelling may last 24–48 hours
Treatment plan
A series may be recommended
Consultation
Required

What it supports

Goals worth discussing clearly.

  • Fine lines
  • Uneven texture
  • Skin firmness
  • Overall vitality

Platelet-rich plasma is prepared from a small blood sample using a centrifuge. The platelet-rich portion is then placed into selected skin areas to support the body’s normal repair signalling.

Because the treatment uses your own plasma, it does not add synthetic filler volume. Changes are gradual and vary with age, skin condition, health, and treatment plan.

Your appointment

A considered process, step by step.

  1. 01

    Assess

    Your health history, skin concerns, and candidacy for blood draw and injection are reviewed.

  2. 02

    Prepare

    A small blood sample is drawn and processed to separate platelet-rich plasma.

  3. 03

    Treat

    The prepared plasma is introduced into targeted areas using the selected protocol.

  4. 04

    Renew

    Redness settles while collagen-related changes develop over the following weeks.

Potential benefits

What this treatment may support.

Benefits describe treatment goals, not guaranteed outcomes. Your provider will explain what is realistic for you.

  • Uses plasma prepared from your own blood
  • May support collagen and texture
  • No synthetic filler is added
  • Can be paired with selected skin protocols when appropriate

Before you book

Candidacy, preparation, and care after.

01

Who it may suit

Candidacy depends on health history, blood and platelet considerations, medications, skin condition, and treatment goals. A consultation is required.

02

How to prepare

Review medications and supplements with the provider, hydrate as directed, and arrive with clean skin. Do not stop prescribed medication without medical advice.

03

Downtime & response

Mild redness, swelling, tenderness, or bruising can occur. Anima’s current page describes a typical return to routine within 24–48 hours.

04

Aftercare

Keep the area clean, use only approved products, protect skin from sun, and follow the provider’s instructions about exercise, heat, and makeup.

Consultation matters: A consultation is required to confirm suitability, treatment scope, expected downtime, and current pricing.

Your questions, answered

PRP Skin Rejuvenation FAQ.

01What is PRP?

Platelet-rich plasma is a concentrated portion of your own blood prepared after a small blood draw.

02What can it support?

Anima describes PRP as a treatment for skin texture, firmness, fine lines, and overall skin vitality.

03Can it be combined with microneedling?

Anima notes that PRP is sometimes paired with microneedling. Suitability and sequence are confirmed in consultation.

04How much downtime is typical?

Mild redness or swelling may last one to two days, although bruising and individual recovery can vary.

05Will I see an immediate result?

Some early change may reflect hydration or swelling. Collagen-related improvement develops gradually over weeks.
