Permanent makeup · Pigment within the lash line

Lash Line Enhancement defined at the root.

A semi-permanent cosmetic tattoo that places fine pigment between the natural lashes so the root line looks denser, without the drawn edge of traditional eyeliner.

What it supports

Goals worth discussing clearly.

  • Light or blonde lashes
  • A thin-looking lash line
  • Smudging eyeliner or mascara
  • Definition without daily makeup

Lash line enhancement places medical-grade pigment within the lash line itself, so the effect reads as denser root growth rather than as liner sitting on the eyelid. Anima’s current page describes tone as customizable, from a soft deep brown to a solid black matched to natural lash colour.

It is cosmetic tattooing on a delicate area. Pigment fades over time and the healed line looks softer than it does immediately after treatment, so longevity varies between clients and cannot be guaranteed.

Your appointment

A considered process, step by step.

  1. 01

    Consult

    Eye health, lash condition, medical history, and the tone you want are reviewed and colour matched.

  2. 02

    Numb

    Numbing cream is applied to the eyelid area before any pigment is placed.

  3. 03

    Enhance

    Pigment is worked in fine points along the lash line to build the agreed density.

  4. 04

    Settle

    Mild puffiness eases over the following days, and a follow-up is planned once the area has healed.

Potential benefits

What this treatment may support.

Benefits describe treatment goals, not guaranteed outcomes. Your provider will explain what is realistic for you.

  • Definition that stays inside the lash line
  • Tone matched to natural lash colour
  • Does not smudge or transfer like eye makeup
  • Less daily eye-makeup time

Before you book

Candidacy, preparation, and care after.

01

Who it may suit

May suit adults with light, sparse, or thinning lashes who want a more defined root line. Eye infection, irritation, recent eye surgery, dry-eye conditions, contact lens tolerance, pregnancy, medicines, allergies, and previous eyelid pigment must all be assessed before booking.

02

How to prepare

Disclose eye conditions, prior reactions, medicines, and recent eye procedures. Anima requires eyelash extensions to be removed at least three days before the session, and they cannot be reapplied until two weeks after the area is fully healed.

03

Downtime & response

Anima lists one to two days of mild, temporary eyelid puffiness. Tenderness, dryness, or light flaking along the lash line can also occur while the pigment settles.

04

Aftercare

Follow the artist’s exact eye-area instructions, keep the lash line clean and dry as directed, avoid rubbing or picking, and delay eye makeup and extensions until cleared. Seek prompt medical advice for vision changes, spreading swelling, or possible signs of infection.

Consultation matters: A consultation is required to confirm suitability, treatment scope, expected downtime, and current pricing.

Your questions, answered

Lash Line Enhancement FAQ.

01Does it look like eyeliner?

Pigment is placed inside the lash row rather than on top of the lid, so the intended effect is a denser-looking lash line instead of a drawn liner shape.

02Does it hurt so close to the eye?

Numbing cream is applied to the eyelid area beforehand. Anima reports most clients describe only a light tickling sensation, although comfort varies.

03Will it damage my natural lashes?

Anima states the pigment is placed superficially in the upper layers of skin and is not intended to disturb lash follicles. Raise any lash or eye concern before treatment.

04Can I keep my lash extensions on?

No. Anima requires extensions to be removed at least three days before the appointment, and they cannot be reapplied until two weeks after full healing.

05How long do results last?

Anima lists roughly two to four years, with a follow-up 4 to 6 weeks after the first visit and maintenance suggested every two to three years. Individual fading varies.
