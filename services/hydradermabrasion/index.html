Skin & facial rejuvenation · Hydration & resurfacing facial

Hydradermabrasion cleansed and replenished.

A multi-step facial that combines fluid-based exfoliation, pore cleansing, and topical hydration for a refreshed look with little interruption.

What it supports

Goals worth discussing clearly.

  • Dehydration
  • Congestion
  • Dullness
  • Rough surface texture

Hydradermabrasion uses a device-assisted, fluid-based approach to exfoliate surface buildup, cleanse pores, and apply selected hydrating solutions.

The treatment is adjusted to your skin’s condition and sensitivity. It can be used as occasional event preparation or as part of a broader maintenance plan.

Your appointment

A considered process, step by step.

  1. 01

    Read the skin

    Current hydration, congestion, sensitivity, and active conditions are reviewed.

  2. 02

    Cleanse

    Surface debris and makeup are removed before device treatment.

  3. 03

    Resurface

    The handpiece combines controlled exfoliation and fluid extraction.

  4. 04

    Replenish

    Selected serums, moisturizer, and sun protection complete the facial.

Potential benefits

What this treatment may support.

Benefits describe treatment goals, not guaranteed outcomes. Your provider will explain what is realistic for you.

  • Combines cleansing and hydration
  • May improve the look of congestion
  • Gentle surface exfoliation
  • Minimal downtime for many skin types

Before you book

Candidacy, preparation, and care after.

01

Who it may suit

Often suitable for people seeking hydration and surface cleansing, but active rash, infection, significant irritation, recent procedures, or ingredient sensitivities must be reviewed.

02

How to prepare

Avoid unapproved exfoliation or aggressive actives before the appointment and share recent procedures, prescriptions, allergies, and current skin irritation.

03

Downtime & response

Most clients have little downtime. Temporary pinkness, sensitivity, or a tight feeling can occur.

04

Aftercare

Keep skincare gentle, avoid unnecessary exfoliation, hydrate the skin, and use broad-spectrum SPF.

Consultation matters: A consultation is required to confirm suitability, treatment scope, expected downtime, and current pricing.

Your questions, answered

Hydradermabrasion FAQ.

01How is hydradermabrasion different from a traditional facial?

It adds a device-assisted fluid exfoliation and extraction step to cleansing and hydration.

02Is it suitable for sensitive skin?

Settings and products can be adjusted, but reactive or compromised skin still requires assessment.

03Is there downtime?

Anima describes little to no downtime, although temporary pinkness or sensitivity is possible.

04Can it help congestion?

The treatment may support surface cleansing and the appearance of congested pores; active acne may need a different plan.

05How often should I book it?

Frequency depends on your skin condition, home routine, goals, and other treatments.
