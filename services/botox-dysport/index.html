Botox® / Dysport® softly considered.

A consultation-led neuromodulator treatment designed to soften selected expression lines while preserving the character and movement that make your face your own.

What it supports

Goals worth discussing clearly.

  • Forehead lines
  • Frown lines
  • Crow’s feet
  • A more rested appearance

Botox® and Dysport® are botulinum toxin treatments that temporarily reduce activity in carefully selected facial muscles. The aim is not to erase expression, but to soften dynamic lines created by repeated movement.

Placement and dose depend on your facial anatomy, natural movement, and preferred level of change. Anima begins with an assessment so the plan can remain precise and conservative.

What this treatment may support.

  • Plan can be adjusted to your facial movement

Before you book

Candidacy, preparation, and care after.

01

Who it may suit

May suit adults who want to soften movement-related lines and are medically appropriate for a neuromodulator. Pregnancy, breastfeeding, certain neuromuscular conditions, medications, and prior reactions must be discussed.

02

How to prepare

Arrive with a clean face when possible and share your medications, supplements, allergies, previous injectable treatments, and upcoming events during consultation.

03

Downtime & response

Temporary redness, tenderness, or small raised areas can occur at injection points. Follow the clinic’s positioning, exercise, skincare, and medication guidance.

04

Aftercare

Avoid rubbing or pressing the treated areas and follow the written instructions provided by your injector. Contact the clinic promptly with any unexpected symptoms.

Consultation matters: A consultation is required to confirm suitability, treatment scope, expected downtime, and current pricing.

Your questions, answered

Botox / Dysport FAQ.

01Will I look frozen?

The plan is built around your preferred level of movement. Anima’s stated approach is to soften expression lines while maintaining a natural-looking result.

02When should I expect a change?

The current Anima page says changes may begin within a few days, with the full effect developing around two weeks. Individual response varies.

03How long can the effect last?

Anima advises that results commonly last around three to four months, although metabolism, dose, area, and individual response affect longevity.

04Is there downtime?

Most people return to routine quickly. Mild redness, tenderness, or small bumps at injection points can occur temporarily.

05How do I know which product is right for me?

Your injector will compare Botox® and Dysport® in the context of your anatomy, goals, history, and treatment area.
