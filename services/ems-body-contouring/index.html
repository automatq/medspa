Wellness & body · Non-invasive muscle stimulation

EMS Body Contouring activated with purpose.

A non-invasive electrical muscle stimulation service designed to create repeated muscle contractions in selected body areas as part of a realistic body-care plan.

What it supports

Goals worth discussing clearly.

  • Muscle activation
  • Subtle definition goals
  • Core or glute-focused support
  • Non-invasive body-care options

EMS body contouring uses externally applied pads or applicators to stimulate repeated muscle contractions. The service is positioned for muscle activation and subtle definition, not weight loss.

Results depend on treatment intensity, series consistency, baseline conditioning, nutrition, activity, and individual response.

Your appointment

A considered process, step by step.

  1. 01

    Screen

    Medical history, implants, treatment area, comfort, and goals are reviewed.

  2. 02

    Position

    Applicators are placed over the selected muscle group.

  3. 03

    Stimulate

    Intensity is increased gradually to create controlled contractions.

  4. 04

    Progress

    Muscle response and comfort are reviewed across the recommended series.

Potential benefits

What this treatment may support.

Benefits describe treatment goals, not guaranteed outcomes. Your provider will explain what is realistic for you.

  • Non-invasive muscle stimulation
  • No incisions or injections
  • Can target selected body areas
  • Little interruption to routine

Before you book

Candidacy, preparation, and care after.

01

Who it may suit

Eligibility depends on medical history and the device. Implanted electrical devices, certain heart or neurologic conditions, pregnancy, recent surgery, and other factors may preclude treatment.

02

How to prepare

Disclose all implanted devices, medical conditions, surgeries, medicines, and pregnancy status. Follow hydration, clothing, and meal-timing directions.

03

Downtime & response

There is usually no planned downtime, but muscle fatigue, tenderness, cramping, or soreness can occur.

04

Aftercare

Hydrate, return to activity gradually, and follow device-specific guidance. Report pain, persistent cramping, weakness, or unexpected symptoms.

Consultation matters: A consultation is required to confirm suitability, treatment scope, expected downtime, and current pricing.

Your questions, answered

EMS Body Contouring FAQ.

01What does EMS stand for?

Electrical muscle stimulation. The device creates controlled contractions in the selected muscle group.

02Is EMS a weight-loss treatment?

No. Anima positions this service for muscle activation and subtle definition, not as a replacement for weight management.

03Will I feel sore?

Muscle fatigue or workout-like soreness can occur after treatment.

04How many sessions will I need?

A series is commonly recommended, but the exact number and schedule depend on the device and your response.

05Who should avoid EMS?

People with certain implanted devices or medical conditions may not be candidates. A complete screening is required.
