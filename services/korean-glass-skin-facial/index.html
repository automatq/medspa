Skin & facial rejuvenation · Layered hydration facial

Korean Glass Skin Facial luminous by design.

A customized facial built around careful exfoliation, hydration, and layered topical care for skin that looks smooth, fresh, and light-reflective.

What it supports

Goals worth discussing clearly.

  • Dehydration
  • Dullness
  • Uneven surface texture
  • Pre-event skin preparation

The 'glass skin' name describes a finish rather than a permanent skin type. Anima’s facial combines customized cleansing, exfoliation, hydration, and selected finishing products.

Products and intensity are adjusted for sensitivity and current skin condition. Results and longevity depend on skin health, home care, and environmental factors.

Your appointment

A considered process, step by step.

  1. 01

    Consult

    Skin condition, sensitivities, routine, and desired finish are discussed.

  2. 02

    Refine

  3. 03

  4. 04

Potential benefits

What this treatment may support.

Benefits describe treatment goals, not guaranteed outcomes. Your provider will explain what is realistic for you.

  • Hydration-focused facial
  • May create a smoother-looking surface
  • Customized to current skin condition
  • Minimal interruption for most clients

Before you book

Candidacy, preparation, and care after.

01

Who it may suit

May suit dry, dull, or uneven-looking skin. Active irritation, infection, allergy, or recent intensive procedures can change the treatment plan.

02

How to prepare

Keep the skin calm, avoid unapproved exfoliation, and bring details of recent treatments, prescriptions, sensitivities, and current products.

03

Downtime & response

Usually minimal. Reactive skin may have temporary pinkness or sensitivity.

04

Aftercare

Use gentle hydration, avoid over-exfoliation, and protect the result with broad-spectrum sun protection.

Consultation matters: A consultation is required to confirm suitability, treatment scope, expected downtime, and current pricing.

Your questions, answered

Korean Glass Skin Facial FAQ.

01What does 'glass skin' mean?

It refers to a hydrated, smooth, luminous-looking finish, not a permanent or poreless skin condition.

02Is this facial suitable before an event?

It may be, but book with enough lead time to account for your skin’s individual response.

03Can sensitive skin receive it?

Products and steps can be adapted, but active sensitivity or barrier damage must be assessed.

04How long does the glow last?

Longevity varies with skin condition, home care, climate, and the specific protocol used.

05Is there downtime?

The treatment is designed for minimal downtime, though temporary redness is possible.
