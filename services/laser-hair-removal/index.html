Laser treatments · Face & body hair reduction

Laser Hair Removal less maintenance.

A light-based treatment planned by hair colour, growth cycle, skin tone, and body area to progressively reduce eligible unwanted hair.

What it supports

Goals worth discussing clearly.

  • Unwanted face or body hair
  • Frequent shaving
  • Ingrown-hair tendency
  • Long-term maintenance reduction

Laser hair removal directs light energy toward pigment in eligible hair follicles. Hair grows in cycles, so a series is required to reach follicles during treatable growth phases.

Hair colour, skin tone, hormonal factors, area, device settings, and prior hair-removal methods all influence response. Fine, light, grey, white, or red hair may respond poorly.

Your appointment

A considered process, step by step.

  1. 01

    Assess

    Hair, skin tone, health history, sun exposure, medicines, and area are reviewed.

  2. 02

  3. 03

  4. 04

    Repeat

    Sessions are spaced around growth cycles, with maintenance considered after the initial series.

Potential benefits

What this treatment may support.

Benefits describe treatment goals, not guaranteed outcomes. Your provider will explain what is realistic for you.

  • Progressive reduction of eligible hair
  • Treats many face and body areas
  • May reduce shaving-related irritation
  • Settings are tailored to skin and hair

Before you book

Candidacy, preparation, and care after.

01

Who it may suit

Best candidacy depends on contrast between hair pigment and skin, device capabilities, medical history, medications, tanning, and the treatment area.

02

How to prepare

Avoid tanning and unapproved light-sensitive products, shave as directed, and do not wax or tweeze during the treatment series unless the clinic advises otherwise.

03

Downtime & response

Temporary redness, warmth, swelling around follicles, sensitivity, or pigment change can occur. Sun exposure increases risk.

04

Aftercare

Cool and protect the skin as directed, avoid heat and friction temporarily, and use diligent broad-spectrum sun protection on exposed areas.

Consultation matters: A consultation is required to confirm suitability, treatment scope, expected downtime, and current pricing.

Your questions, answered

Laser Hair Removal FAQ.

01Why are multiple sessions needed?

Only follicles in certain growth phases respond at a given session, so treatment is spaced across several cycles.

02Which areas does Anima treat?

The current page lists common face and body areas. Exact areas and appointment times are confirmed in booking.

03Can every hair colour be treated?

No. Hair with little pigment, including white, grey, and some very light or red hair, may not respond well.

04Can I wax between sessions?

Waxing and tweezing remove the target from the follicle and are usually avoided during a series. Follow the clinic’s instructions.

05Is the reduction permanent?

Laser is generally described as long-term hair reduction. Hormones and new follicle activity can make maintenance necessary.
