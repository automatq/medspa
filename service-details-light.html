Treatment guide

What to expect from your treatment.

A plain-language guide to consultation, preparation, downtime, and maintenance. Your provider will confirm instructions for the treatment you choose.

The processInjectablesSkinLaserWellness & bodyBeauty

The Anima process

Four steps, with room for every question.

01

Consult

Share your goals, health history, current routine, and expectations.

02

Plan

Review suitable options, tradeoffs, pricing, timing, and expected outcomes.

03

Treat

Proceed only when you understand the plan and feel comfortable with it.

04

Support

Follow personalized aftercare and return for review or maintenance as advised.

01

Injectable treatments

Injectables require an individual consultation. Your plan may depend on anatomy, treatment history, product amount, health history, and the outcome you want.

Before

Bring medication, allergy, and prior treatment details. Ask about anything that could affect bruising or healing.

After

Temporary swelling, redness, tenderness, or bruising can occur. Follow the instructions provided for your specific treatment.

Results

Timing, longevity, and the number of sessions vary. Results cannot be guaranteed and maintenance may be recommended.

02

Skin & facial rejuvenation

Gentle facials may involve little or no downtime. More intensive options, including microneedling with PDRN, can cause short-term redness or sensitivity.

Before

Share your skincare routine, recent procedures, sensitivities, and current skin concerns.

After

Some treatments may cause redness or sensitivity for 24–48 hours. Use only the aftercare products and sun protection advised.

Results

Hydration and surface glow may appear quickly; texture and collagen goals often need time and a planned series.

03

Laser treatments

Laser suitability depends on the target, treatment area, skin characteristics, sun exposure, and other factors discussed during assessment.

Before

A consultation or patch test may be appropriate. Follow instructions about sun exposure, hair removal, and skincare.

After

Temporary warmth, redness, or sensitivity can occur. Sun protection and treatment-specific aftercare are important.

Results

Laser treatments commonly require a series. Response and spacing vary by treatment goal and individual factors.

04

Wellness & body treatments

Weight management and EMS body contouring are supportive services, not promises of a particular outcome. Eligibility and expectations are reviewed before starting.

Before

Be ready to discuss relevant health history, current medications, goals, and previous approaches.

During

Your provider will explain the proposed schedule, what the treatment can support, and when it may not be suitable.

Results

Individual response varies. Sustainable outcomes may involve broader lifestyle and medical considerations.

05

Beauty enhancements

Brows and lashes are planned around your natural features, desired definition, maintenance preferences, and skin or hair considerations.

Before

Share sensitivities, previous reactions, and recent brow or lash services.

After

Follow service-specific instructions about moisture, products, and touch-ups.

Maintenance

Longevity and refresh cycles vary by service, aftercare, hair growth, and skin characteristics.

Important: this guide is general education, not medical advice. A consultation is needed to assess suitability, risks, contraindications, aftercare, and expected outcomes. Current pricing and appointment duration appear in the live booking schedule.
